Frequently asked
questions.

Common questions about our litter, toppers and partnerships.

Why choose cassava litter instead of clay?

Cassava is a renewable crop: plant-based, biodegradable and gentle on paws. Clay and bentonite are mined minerals that stay in landfills and tend to be dusty.

Which fragrances and sizes are available?

Three fragrances: Fragrance-Free, Lemongrass and Lavender. Three sizes: 4.4 lb (2 kg), 8.8 lb (4 kg) and 13.2 lb (6 kg).

What are the Natural Flavor Booster toppers?

High-protein meal toppers for dogs made with cassava and Meat, Chicken or Liver. Crunchy, 100% natural, with no artificial preservatives or flavorings, in 0.2 lb (100 g), 0.4 lb (200 g) and 0.8 lb (400 g).

Can I sell Amitys products under my own brand?

Yes. Our private label is a full service solution: formula R&D, packaging development and production, with certified quality.

How do I become an authorized distributor?

Get in touch with your regional desk, receive the catalog, samples, price list and container lead times, then launch with our export logistics and marketing support.

Who handles my region?

Ricardo covers the USA & Canada, Guilherme and Luiz cover Latin America & Asia, and Erica covers Brazil.
